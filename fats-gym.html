About us

A battle-tested home for the craft

Fats Gym has been part of Manchester boxing since 1995. Founded by a coach who spent twelve years as a professional under Oliver & Humphrey Harrison - former British Muay Thai champion and English international - the gym is now a community interest company: same hard training, bigger heart.

  • Founded in 1995 - three decades of Manchester boxing
  • Non-profit community interest company since April 2025
  • Founder: 12-year pro career under Oliver & Humphrey Harrison
  • Ring, heavy bags, speed bags, cardio and weights
