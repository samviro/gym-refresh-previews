About us

A historic home for Birmingham boxers

Tracing its origins back to 1930 as Small Heath Boxing Club, PBBA is one of the UK's most historic boxing institutions, named after legendary Irish boxing coach Pat Benson MBE and running schools and community programmes across Birmingham.
