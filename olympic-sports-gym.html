About us

A high-performance home for strength athletes

Established in 1983 by Mick Clegg, Strength & Conditioning Coach at Manchester United from 2000 to 2011, Olympic Sports Gym has hosted world stars including Cristiano Ronaldo, Wayne Rooney and Roy Keane. Today it remains one of the North West's most serious free-weights facilities.
